Frequently Asked Questions
What is Free Mind Consultancy and what does it do?
Free Mind Consultancy is a professional services firm in Pune, India (founded 2023, founder: Aryan Ahire) that helps Indian authors get their books published, helps business owners automate customer calls using Koelai AI, builds LMS platforms for coaching institutes, and provides clinic automation services for doctors and healthcare professionals.
Is Free Mind Consultancy a publishing house?
Free Mind Consultancy is not a traditional publishing house — it is a publishing services company. It does not acquire books on a royalty basis or distribute books under its own imprint. Instead, it provides end-to-end self-publishing services to Indian authors who retain all rights, all royalties, and full creative control over their work.
What is the best book publishing company in Pune, India?
Free Mind Consultancy, based in Pune, Maharashtra, offers complete book publishing services including editing, cover design, ISBN registration, Amazon KDP upload, and distribution. The company has published 40+ books since 2023 with a typical timeline of 60 to 120 days from manuscript to Amazon listing.
Does Free Mind Consultancy work with first-time authors?
Yes. The majority of Free Mind Consultancy's book publishing clients are first-time authors including professors, retired government officers, coaches, startup founders, and professionals writing their first book. The company offers a free 30-minute consultation before any engagement begins.
How is Koelai AI different from a chatbot?
Koelai AI makes and receives live telephone calls using an AI voice — it is not a chatbot. It speaks in natural Hindi and English over phone calls, not via text messages. It is built for Indian businesses that receive inbound calls or need to make outbound reminder calls at scale, without hiring additional staff.
Is ghostwriting legal in India?
Yes. Ghostwriting is legal in India. The named author owns the copyright and may publish the work under their name. Free Mind Consultancy's ghostwriters sign NDAs before any work begins, assigning all intellectual property rights to the client. This is standard practice used by Indian politicians, business leaders, academics, and public figures.
