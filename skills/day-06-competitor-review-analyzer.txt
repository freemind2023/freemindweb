# Competitor Review Analyzer
From "100 Days of Marketing Engineering" - Day 6 - Free Mind Consultancy (freemindconsult.com)

## How to use this file
- Fastest: start a new Claude chat (or a Project) and paste this whole file as the first message, then paste your reviews.
- Or: save it as SKILL.md inside a folder named competitor-review-analyzer and upload the folder as a skill in Claude settings (Capabilities > Skills).

## What you need from the user
1. Their business in one sentence.
2. The competitor's name.
3. Reviews: pasted text, ideally 30+ reviews, with star ratings and dates if available. If the user gives only a link and you cannot open it, ask them to paste the reviews. Never invent reviews.

## Steps
1. Clean the data. Ignore duplicates and obvious spam. Note the count and date range you actually received.
2. Group complaints into themes (for example: price, support, delivery, quality, onboarding, reliability, trust). Count how many reviews mention each theme.
3. Separate three things: what customers HATE (complaints), what they EXPECTED (unmet promises) and what they WANT FIXED (requests).
4. Pull 2-3 short verbatim quotes per top theme. Quote exactly. Do not paraphrase inside quote marks.
5. Convert themes into marketing material for the user's business:
   - 5 ad angles (hook + one-line promise)
   - 3 landing page section ideas
   - 3 offer or guarantee ideas that answer the top complaints
6. Flag risks: small sample, only 1-star reviews (biased), old reviews, possible fake reviews.

## Output format
- Summary (3 lines)
- Theme table: theme | number of reviews | example quote
- Ad angles, landing page ideas, offers
- Confidence and limits

## Rules
- Use only the reviews provided. Do not guess ratings, traffic or revenue.
- Do not copy the competitor's wording into ads; use the insight, not their text.
- If evidence for a theme is thin (1-2 reviews), say so.
