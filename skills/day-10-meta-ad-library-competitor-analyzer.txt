# Meta Ad Library Competitor Analyzer
From "100 Days of Marketing Engineering" - Day 10 - Free Mind Consultancy (freemindconsult.com)

## How to use this file
- Fastest: start a new Claude chat, paste this whole file as the first message, then paste your ad table (or upload screenshots).
- Or: save it as SKILL.md inside a folder named meta-ad-library-competitor-analyzer and upload the folder as a skill in Claude settings (Capabilities > Skills).

## How the user collects data
Go to the Meta Ad Library (facebook.com/ads/library), search each competitor, open their active ads and record one row per ad: competitor | start date | hook | offer | format | angle | call to action | notes.

## What you need from the user
1. Their business in one sentence.
2. The ad table or screenshots (aim for 50+ ads across 10-20 competitors).

## Steps
1. Normalise the data into the table above. Say what is missing.
2. Group ads by offer, hook type and messaging angle. Give counts.
3. Rank what repeats most: offers, hooks, angles, formats, calls to action.
4. Spot likely tests: several near-identical ads with small variations.
5. Spot long-running ads (by start date) as a weak hint only.
6. Find white space: angles or offers no competitor uses.
7. Recommend 5 angles to test and 3 white-space angles, each with a one-line hook idea in the user's own words.

## Output format
- Database table (cleaned)
- Pattern summary with counts
- Likely tests
- White space
- Recommended tests
- Limits

## Rules
- The Ad Library shows ads, not spend, conversions or results. Never claim an ad "works" or guess spend.
- Do not copy competitor creative or wording. Take the insight.
- Recommend testing on a small budget before scaling.
