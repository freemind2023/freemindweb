Frequently Asked Questions

Do you use the official WhatsApp Business API?

Yes, where a business needs it for higher volume or verified messaging; for smaller-scale needs, simpler automation may be sufficient. We'll recommend the right approach for your actual volume and budget.

Which CRM do you work with?

Whatever CRM a business already uses or is a good fit for its size and budget — we can also set up lightweight automation connecting WhatsApp, forms, and spreadsheets for businesses that don't need a full CRM yet.

What does "lead routing" actually mean?

A new inquiry — from a form, an ad, or WhatsApp — is automatically assigned to the right person or follow-up sequence based on rules you define, instead of sitting in an inbox.

Is this only for clinics, or for any business?

Free Mind already runs WhatsApp automation for clinics as a dedicated vertical — this broader service applies the same discipline to any business.
