AEO & GEO Services

AEO Services India: Get Cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity & AI Overviews

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is the practice of structuring a business's content so AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews can find it, understand it, and cite it when someone asks a question that business can answer.

Free Mind helps Indian businesses build that technical foundation — from Pune, for clients across India and internationally. This is technical AI-readiness work, not a promise of appearing in any specific AI answer; no credible consultancy can guarantee that.

AEO vs. GEO vs. SEO

The three overlap heavily in the technical work involved, but they optimize for different outcomes:

  SEO AEO GEO
Goal Rank in a list of search results Get extracted as a direct answer (featured snippets, voice, AI Overviews) Get found, understood and cited by generative AI (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini)
Primary surface Google/Bing results page Snippets, voice assistants, AI Overviews AI chat answers and their citations
Depends on Keywords, backlinks, Core Web Vitals Clear, self-contained answers; logical headings; FAQ content AI-crawler accessibility, structured data, consistent entity facts across the web
Can you measure it? Yes — rank tracking, Search Console Partly — snippet/AI Overview appearance Yes — AI-visibility tracking (what we use Vimarsh for, below)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is AEO?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is the practice of structuring content so AI assistants like ChatGPT and Perplexity cite your business when users ask questions your industry can answer.

What's the difference between AEO, GEO and SEO?

SEO ranks you in a list of search results. AEO gets you extracted as a direct answer (snippets, voice, AI Overviews). GEO is about being found, understood and cited by generative AI like ChatGPT and Perplexity. See the comparison table above for the full breakdown.

How do I check if ChatGPT mentions my business?

Use Vimarsh, Free Mind's own AI-visibility tool — it runs real prompts against ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity and shows you whether, how, and how often your business comes up, including the exact response text.

How long do AEO results take?

Technical readiness work (schema, crawler access, content restructuring) can be implemented within days to weeks. Whether and when an AI system actually starts citing you afterward isn't something any outside party controls or can promise a timeline for.

How much do AEO services cost in India?

It depends on your site's current size and AI-readiness — this isn't a fixed-scope service like ISBN registration, so we don't publish a flat number. Book a free consultation for a scoped quote.

Can you guarantee my business will be cited by an AI system?

No. No credible consultancy can guarantee inclusion in a specific AI system's generated answers. We deliver technical AI-readiness — the things within a website's control — and are explicit that readiness isn't the same as a guaranteed citation.

How is this different from traditional SEO?

Traditional SEO ranks you in a list of results. AEO and GEO are about being understood and potentially quoted directly. Good technical SEO is a foundation for both, but AEO/GEO adds entity consistency and answer-first formatting that classic SEO doesn't always prioritize.
