AEO & GEO Services

Make Your Business Easy for AI Systems to Find, Understand and Cite

Free Mind helps Indian businesses build the technical foundation that AI answer engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews — rely on to understand a business and, where appropriate, cite it. This is technical readiness work, not a promise of appearing in any specific AI answer.

What is AEO?

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of structuring content so it can be directly extracted as an answer — by Google's featured snippets, AI Overviews, or voice assistants. It depends on clear, self-contained answers, logical headings, and genuinely useful FAQ content.

What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of making a website's content and entity information easy for generative AI systems — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude — to find, understand, and potentially cite. It depends on technical AI-crawler accessibility, structured data, and consistent factual information about a business.

What This Covers

Structured Data & Entity Consistency

Schema markup and consistent facts about your business across every page — the raw material AI systems use to build an accurate picture of who you are.

AI Crawler Accessibility

Confirming AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, and others) can actually access and read your content.

Direct-Answer Content

Restructuring key pages so genuine user questions get a clear, extractable answer near the top — not buried in marketing copy.

Platform-Specific Considerations

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews each behave differently — the technical foundation overlaps heavily, but we're explicit about what's shared work versus platform-specific nuance.

Who This Is For

Founders and businesses who've noticed AI assistants are increasingly answering the questions that used to send someone to Google — and want their business's technical foundation in order for that shift, without paying for an unrealistic guarantee.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this the same for ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews?

The underlying technical readiness work overlaps significantly — clean structured data, crawler accessibility, and clear direct-answer content help across the board. Each platform has its own behavior that no outside party fully controls.

Can you guarantee my business will be cited by an AI system?

No. No credible consultancy can guarantee inclusion in a specific AI system's generated answers. We deliver technical AI-readiness — the things within a website's control — and are explicit that readiness isn't the same as a guaranteed citation.

How is this different from traditional SEO?

Traditional SEO ranks you in a list of results. AEO and GEO are about being understood and potentially quoted directly. Good technical SEO is a foundation for both, but AEO/GEO adds entity consistency and answer-first formatting that classic SEO doesn't always prioritize.
