AI Chatbot Development

A Chatbot That Knows Your Business — and Knows When to Hand Off to a Human

Free Mind builds custom chatbots trained on your own FAQs, docs, and product information — for your website or WhatsApp — designed to answer accurately, qualify interested visitors, and pass anything it can't handle to a real person.

Book Free Consultation WhatsApp Us

The Problem

Visitors ask the same handful of questions repeatedly, often outside business hours, and either wait for a reply or leave. A generic chatbot widget that doesn't actually know your business ends up frustrating people more than it helps.

What We Do

We build chatbots grounded in your actual knowledge base — your FAQs, product docs, and policies — so answers come from real information, not a generic model guessing. We design a clear hand-off path to a human for anything outside the bot's scope, and can deploy it on your website, WhatsApp, or both.

Use Cases

  • Answering common product/service questions 24/7, on your website or WhatsApp
  • Qualifying leads with a few questions before routing them to sales
  • Reducing repetitive support tickets by handling FAQs automatically
  • Guiding a visitor toward the right service page or contact path

How It Works

  1. Gather your knowledge base — FAQs, docs, policies, product details.
  2. Design the conversation — what it should ask, answer, and when to hand off.
  3. Build and test — including questions it should correctly decline to answer.
  4. Deploy and monitor — on your website and/or WhatsApp, with visibility into real conversations.

What You Get

A working chatbot trained on your content, deployed where your customers actually are, with a defined human hand-off path — not a black box you can't inspect.

Who This Is For

Businesses fielding repetitive questions through a website or WhatsApp, who want faster responses without giving up accuracy or losing the human touch for anything that genuinely needs it.

Example Scenario

Illustrative, not a specific client case: a service business gets the same pricing and availability questions repeatedly, often after hours. A trained chatbot can answer those instantly, and if a visitor asks something outside its knowledge, it hands off to WhatsApp for a human reply the next morning — instead of the visitor just leaving.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will the chatbot only answer from our own information, or make things up?

We build chatbots grounded in your own knowledge base specifically to reduce the risk of invented answers — it's designed to say it doesn't know rather than guess.

Can the chatbot qualify leads before a salesperson gets involved?

Yes — asking a few qualifying questions and routing only genuinely interested visitors to your team is a common use case.

Where can the chatbot live — website, WhatsApp, both?

Both are possible depending on where your customers actually are — many Indian businesses see more engagement on WhatsApp than a website widget.

What happens when the chatbot can't answer a question?

It hands off to a human rather than guessing or leaving the customer stuck.
