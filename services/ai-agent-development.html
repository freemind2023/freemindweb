AI Agent Development

Workflows That Reason Through Multiple Steps — Not Just Reply to a Message

An AI agent goes further than a chatbot — it can look things up, use tools, and follow a multi-step process, acting within permissions you define. Free Mind builds these for specific business workflows, with human approval steps built in wherever the stakes call for it.

Book Free Consultation WhatsApp Us

The Problem

Many multi-step business tasks — qualifying a lead across several data points, researching and compiling a report, following up across multiple touchpoints — are too complex for a simple chatbot script but too repetitive to keep doing entirely by hand.

What We Do

We design and build AI agents scoped to a specific, well-defined business process — with clear permissions on what the agent can and can't do on its own, and a human approval step wherever the outcome has real consequences. This often connects with our n8n automation and WhatsApp/CRM work to actually act on the systems you already use.

Use Cases

  • An agent that qualifies inbound leads against your criteria and routes only the qualified ones to sales
  • A research agent that compiles a structured competitor or market summary from multiple sources
  • An agent that drafts a follow-up sequence and flags it for human review before sending
  • A multi-step internal-operations agent that checks several systems and produces one consolidated report

How It Works

  1. Scope the task — the exact process, decision points, and tools involved.
  2. Define permissions — what the agent can do automatically, what needs human approval.
  3. Build and test — with real scenarios, including edge cases.
  4. Deploy with monitoring — so you can see what the agent is doing, not just trust it blindly.

What You Get

A working AI agent scoped to your defined process, with explicit permission boundaries, a human-approval step where it matters, and documentation of what the agent does and doesn't handle on its own.

Who This Is For

Businesses with a specific, repeatable, multi-step process that's too complex for a basic chatbot but well-defined enough to automate responsibly — not businesses looking for a general-purpose "do everything" AI.

Example Scenario

Illustrative, not a specific client case: a B2B business receives inquiries through multiple channels. An AI agent could check each inquiry against qualification criteria, pull relevant company information, and prepare a summary for the sales team — with the actual sales conversation still handled by a person.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between a chatbot and an AI agent?

A chatbot is primarily conversational. An AI agent can reason through a multi-step task, use tools, and follow a defined workflow — potentially taking actions within permissions you set.

Will the AI agent act completely on its own?

Only within permissions and approval steps we design together. For anything with real consequences, we build in a human checkpoint by default.

What tools can an AI agent use?

Depends on the workflow — it can look things up, query a database, call an API, or trigger an automation. We scope this to your actual task.

Do you have an official partnership with any AI agent platform?

We build using established, reputable tools appropriate to each project — we don't claim a partnership unless one genuinely exists.
