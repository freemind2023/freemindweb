Social Media Management

Consistent Social Media Without It Eating Your Week

Content planning, creatives, captions, scheduling, and posting, handled end to end — so your social presence stays consistent without it being the thing that falls off your own to-do list every week.

Book Free Consultation WhatsApp Us

The Problem

Social media is usually the first thing to slip when a founder or team gets busy — posts become irregular, captions get rushed, and the account stops looking like it belongs to an active business.

What We Do

We plan a content calendar around your actual business and offers, create the posts (graphics, short video edits, captions), and handle scheduling and posting across the platforms you're active on. You review and approve content before it goes live.

Use Cases

  • A founder who wants to post consistently but doesn't have time to plan and create content every week
  • A business whose social accounts have gone quiet and need a consistent cadence restarted
  • A team that wants content planned a month ahead instead of scrambled together daily

How It Works

  1. Plan — a content calendar built around your offers, audience, and any key dates (launches, events).
  2. Create — graphics, captions, and short-form video edits for each planned post.
  3. Review — you approve content before it's scheduled.
  4. Post and report — scheduled posting, with a simple report on what went out and basic engagement.

What You Get

A content calendar, the actual creative assets and captions, scheduled posting, and a basic performance summary. Content volume and platforms covered are agreed upfront based on your needs and budget.

Who This Is For

Founders and businesses who want a consistent social media presence without doing the planning, creative, and posting work themselves.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which platforms do you cover?

Typically Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn, depending on where your audience actually is — we'll confirm which platforms make sense for your business before starting.

Do I review content before it's posted?

Yes — content is planned and created in advance and sent for your approval before it's scheduled.

Do you run ads as part of this?

Social media management covers organic content. Paid campaigns are a separate service — see Meta Ads Management.

How much content do I get per month?

This is agreed upfront based on your needs and budget — there's no single fixed volume across all clients.
