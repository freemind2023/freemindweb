Social Media Management for Authors & Coaches

Social Media That Sells Your Book, Course, or Coaching — Not Just a Generic Feed

Built specifically for authors, coaches, and course creators — content planning, creatives, captions, scheduling, and posting, handled end to end, written around what you're actually selling instead of generic "social media management agency" content that could belong to any business.

The Problem

Authors, coaches, and course creators have a specific social media problem generic agencies don't solve: the content has to do actual work — sell a book, fill a coaching cohort, promote a course launch — not just "look active." Most social media management is built for product or retail brands, and the posts that come out of it read generically no matter who the client is. Meanwhile, social media is usually the first thing to slip when you get busy with the actual writing, coaching, or teaching.

What We Do

We plan a content calendar built around what you're actually promoting — a book launch, a coaching cohort opening, a course enrollment window — not a generic weekly posting schedule. For authors, that means content that sells the book without reading like an ad: excerpts, behind-the-writing posts, launch countdowns. For coaches and course creators, that means content built around your actual program: testimonials, free-value posts that lead into your offer, enrollment-window promotion. We create the posts (graphics, short video edits, captions) and handle scheduling and posting across the platforms you're active on. You review and approve content before it goes live.

Use Cases

  • An author with a book launching in the next few months who needs a content ramp-up, not a generic feed
  • A coach filling a cohort who needs content that actually moves people toward enrolling, not just visibility
  • A course creator whose social accounts have gone quiet between launches and need a consistent cadence restarted

How It Works

  1. Plan — a content calendar built around your offers, audience, and any key dates (launches, events).
  2. Create — graphics, captions, and short-form video edits for each planned post.
  3. Review — you approve content before it's scheduled.
  4. Post and report — scheduled posting, with a simple report on what went out and basic engagement.

What You Get

A content calendar, the actual creative assets and captions, scheduled posting, and a basic performance summary. Content volume and platforms covered are agreed upfront based on your needs and budget.

Who This Is For

Authors, coaches, and course creators who want social media that actually supports what they're selling — a book, a coaching program, a course — without doing the planning, creative, and posting work themselves.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you do social media specifically for authors and coaches?

Yes — this is our focus, not a generic add-on. Content is planned around what you're actually promoting (a book launch, a coaching cohort, a course enrollment window), not a one-size-fits-all content calendar built for retail or product brands.

Which platforms do you cover?

Typically Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn, depending on where your audience actually is — we'll confirm which platforms make sense for your business before starting.

Do I review content before it's posted?

Yes — content is planned and created in advance and sent for your approval before it's scheduled.

Do you run ads as part of this?

Social media management covers organic content. Paid campaigns are a separate service — see Meta Ads Management.

How much content do I get per month?

This is agreed upfront based on your needs and budget — there's no single fixed volume across all clients.
