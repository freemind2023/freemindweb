AI-Integrated HRMS & ERP Systems

HR, Payroll and Operations in One System

A system built around how your business actually runs HR, payroll, attendance and operations — with AI assistance where it genuinely saves time, not bolted on for its own sake.

Book Free Consultation WhatsApp Us

The Problem

Many growing businesses run HR, payroll, attendance and operations across a patchwork of spreadsheets, WhatsApp messages, and disconnected tools. Nothing talks to anything else, and small manual errors compound into bigger problems at month-end.

What We Do

We build an HRMS/ERP system scoped to what your business actually needs: employee records, attendance, payroll calculation, leave management, and operational workflows specific to your business, connected into one system instead of several disconnected ones. Where it genuinely helps, we add AI assistance — for example, flagging attendance anomalies or drafting routine HR communications — with a human still making final decisions.

Use Cases

  • A growing team that has outgrown spreadsheets for HR and payroll
  • A business running multiple disconnected tools for attendance, payroll, and operations
  • An operations team that wants AI-assisted anomaly detection without handing over decisions to a system

How It Works

  1. Scope — map current HR, payroll and operations processes, and what should move into the new system.
  2. Design — a system structure matched to your actual team size and workflows, not an off-the-shelf template.
  3. Build and integrate — development, with integration into existing tools where it makes sense.
  4. Train and hand over — training for your team and documentation for ongoing use.

What You Get

A working HRMS/ERP system scoped to your business, documentation, and training for your team. Scope (which modules — HR, payroll, attendance, broader ERP/operations) is confirmed upfront and priced accordingly.

Who This Is For

Businesses that have outgrown spreadsheets and disconnected tools for HR, payroll and operations, and want one system built around how they actually work.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a ready-made product or a custom build?

Custom, scoped to your business. We don't start from a rigid off-the-shelf template that forces your processes to fit the software.

What does 'AI-integrated' actually mean here?

AI assistance is added only where it genuinely saves time — for example flagging anomalies or drafting routine communications — with a human still making the final call on anything that matters.

Can this replace our existing payroll or attendance tool?

It can, depending on scope — we'll confirm this during the scoping stage rather than assume it upfront.

How is this priced?

Based on the modules and scope agreed during the scoping call — there's no fixed package, since team size and process complexity vary a lot.
