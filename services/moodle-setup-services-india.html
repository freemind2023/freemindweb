Quick Answer

Free Mind Consultancy sets up and manages Moodle — the open-source LMS built for institutional learning — for colleges, universities, corporate L&D teams, training companies, and government skill-development programs across India. Three tiers cover everything from a branded Quickstart install to Enterprise setups with SSO, SIS/HRMS integration, and custom plugin development. We also build the custom integrated applications (mobile apps, system integrations) that go around your Moodle deployment.

We don't just set up Moodle — we build the integrated applications around it

Most institutions eventually need more than the LMS itself: a branded mobile app, a connection into an existing SIS/HRMS, or a custom internal tool. That work is part of our Enterprise/Custom tier and available as a standalone engagement.

  • ✓Native mobile app development — a branded app layer on top of your Moodle instance for students, faculty, or employees.
  • ✓SIS / HRMS integration — connect Moodle to the student information or HR system you already run.
  • ✓Custom plugin & report development — purpose-built Moodle plugins and reporting dashboards for requirements the core platform doesn't cover.
  • ✓Single sign-on & multi-system auth — SAML/LDAP/Google Workspace/Microsoft 365, wired into your existing identity provider.
