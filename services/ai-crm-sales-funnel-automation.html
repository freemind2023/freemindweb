The Problem

A lead often passes through several disconnected steps before a salesperson ever talks to them — sourcing, an initial call or message, manual CRM entry, and follow-up — and each handoff is a place where leads get delayed, duplicated, or dropped entirely.

What We Do

We connect the funnel end to end: leads are sourced or received, an initial qualification step filters and scores them, qualified details are entered into your CRM automatically, follow-up sequences run without manual effort, and a clean handover brings a human salesperson in once a lead is genuinely ready. For WhatsApp-specific automation and CRM setup on their own, see our WhatsApp & CRM Automation service — this page is about connecting that with lead sourcing and qualification into one funnel.

Use Cases

  • A business that wants lead sourcing and qualification connected into its actual CRM and sales process, not running as separate disconnected tools
  • A sales team drowning in manual lead entry and follow-up
  • A founder who wants a clear, automatic handover point from AI-handled qualification to a human closer

How It Works

  1. Map the funnel — your current path from a new lead to a closed deal, and where it breaks down today.
  2. Connect sourcing and qualification — lead sourcing and qualification integrated into the funnel.
  3. Automate CRM and follow-up — qualified lead data flows into your CRM automatically, with automated follow-up sequences.
  4. Define the handover — a clear rule for when a lead is handed to a human salesperson, and how.

What You Get

A connected funnel from lead source through AI-assisted qualification to CRM entry, automated follow-up, and human handover, built around your existing CRM and sales process wherever possible.

Who This Is For

Businesses with real lead volume who want the path from a new lead to a human conversation automated and connected, instead of each step handled manually and separately.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from your WhatsApp & CRM Automation service?

That service focuses on WhatsApp-specific automation and CRM setup. This service connects lead sourcing and qualification (not just WhatsApp) into the same kind of CRM and follow-up automation, as one funnel.

Does automation replace my sales team?

No — automation handles initial contact and qualification; a defined handover brings a human salesperson in once a lead is ready, which is a deliberate part of the design, not a gap.

Which CRM do you work with?

We build around your existing CRM wherever practical rather than forcing a switch to a new one — tell us what you currently use.
