ChatGPT Ads & AI Advertising Management

Advertising on the Platforms Where People Are Now Searching

Strategy and campaign management for advertising inside AI platforms such as ChatGPT, as these advertising products roll out. This is subject to platform availability and account eligibility — not every business or region has access yet, and we'll tell you plainly where that stands before any work begins.

The Problem

AI chat platforms are becoming a real discovery surface, and advertising products on them are rolling out gradually, with eligibility that varies by platform, account, and region. Businesses that want to be positioned early don't have an easy way to track or act on that.

What We Do

We track the rollout of advertising products on AI platforms such as ChatGPT, and where a business is eligible, we handle strategy and campaign management the same way we would for any paid channel — audience, creative, budget, and reporting. Where eligibility or availability doesn't yet exist, we'll say so directly instead of pitching something that isn't actually accessible.

Use Cases

  • A business that wants to be positioned early as AI advertising platforms open up
  • A founder who wants an honest read on whether ChatGPT Ads (or similar) is actually available to them right now
  • A business already investing in AEO/GEO (AI search visibility) that wants paid AI advertising considered alongside it

How It Works

  1. Check eligibility — confirm what's actually available to your account/region at the time of asking.
  2. Strategy — if available, build a campaign strategy around your offer and audience.
  3. Manage — campaign setup, creative, and ongoing management, same discipline as our other paid channels.
  4. Report — plain reporting on what the platform's ad product actually measures.

What You Get

Either a managed campaign (where eligibility exists) or an honest assessment of current availability and a plan for when it opens up. We do not promise access to ad products that aren't currently available.

Who This Is For

Businesses that want to stay ahead on AI advertising platforms as they roll out, with a partner who will say clearly when something isn't available yet rather than oversell it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ChatGPT Ads available right now?

Availability depends on the platform's current rollout, your account, and region — this changes over time. We check and tell you plainly before any work begins, rather than assuming access.

What happens if it's not available for my account?

We'll say so directly. We can still advise on readiness (so you're positioned once it opens) without billing for campaign management that can't run yet.

Is this the same as AEO/GEO (AI search visibility)?

Related but different — AEO/GEO is about appearing in AI-generated answers organically; this service is about paid advertising within AI platforms, where that product exists.

Do you guarantee results on these platforms?

No — these are new, evolving ad products, and we won't promise outcomes before there's a track record to base that on.
