Lead Sourcing & Cold Outreach

A Real List of Prospects, Not a Purchased One

B2B prospect research, lead sourcing, list building, and cold outreach preparation — built around your actual ideal customer profile, not a generic purchased contact list.

Book Free Consultation WhatsApp Us

The Problem

Outbound sales efforts often stall at the first step — building an actual list of relevant prospects — and teams end up either skipping outbound entirely or using a generic, outdated purchased list that converts poorly.

What We Do

We research and build a prospect list matched to your actual ideal customer profile (role, industry, company size, location), and prepare outreach — sequences and messaging — ready for your team to send, or for us to manage depending on scope.

Use Cases

  • A B2B business that wants to start outbound prospecting but has no process or list to start from
  • A sales team that has a product-market fit but hasn't systematized lead sourcing
  • A founder who wants outreach messaging prepared professionally instead of sent ad hoc

How It Works

  1. Define ICP — confirm the ideal customer profile: role, industry, company size, geography.
  2. Source — research and build a list of real, current prospects matching that profile.
  3. Prepare outreach — draft outreach sequences and messaging for your review.
  4. Hand off or manage — your team sends outreach, or we manage sending depending on agreed scope.

What You Get

A prospect list matched to your ICP and prepared outreach messaging/sequences. Whether outreach is sent by your team or managed by us is agreed upfront — this page covers sourcing and preparation either way.

Who This Is For

B2B businesses that want a real, targeted prospect list and professionally prepared outreach instead of guessing or buying a generic contact database.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you send the outreach messages, or do we?

Either — this is agreed upfront. We can prepare the list and messaging for your team to send, or manage sending as part of the engagement.

Where do the leads come from?

Research against your defined ideal customer profile using publicly available business information — not a purchased, generic contact database.

Do you guarantee a number of leads or replies?

No — list quality and outreach can be measured, but response and conversion rates depend on your offer and market, which we don't control.

Is this the same as Meta/Google ads?

No — this is outbound (you reaching out to prospects), not paid advertising. See Meta Ads Management for the paid-ads side.
