Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between SEO and AEO/GEO?

SEO is about ranking well in traditional search results like Google. AEO and GEO are about how AI systems like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews find, understand, and cite your content when answering a question. The two overlap heavily — clean technical SEO and well-organized content help with both.

Can you guarantee my business will be mentioned by ChatGPT or shown in an AI Overview?

No. No credible consultancy can guarantee inclusion in a specific AI system's answers — these are probabilistic systems controlled by third parties. We can make your site's technical AI-readiness as strong as possible and be transparent that this is readiness, not a guarantee of citation.

Do you offer a one-time audit or ongoing work?

Both, depending on what you need — a one-time audit to identify gaps, or ongoing search and AI-visibility work. We'll recommend the right scope after understanding your site and goals.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Free Mind is based in Pune, India, and this service is built around Indian search and business context, but the underlying principles aren't India-specific. Contact us to discuss your situation.
