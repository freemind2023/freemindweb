Meta Business Suite Setup & Verification Support

Get Your Meta Business Setup Actually Right

Facebook and Instagram business setup, Business Portfolio configuration, and account integration, done correctly the first time — plus honest guidance on verification eligibility. We cannot promise Meta verification, since that decision is made by Meta, not us.

The Problem

Meta's business tools (Business Portfolio, Page and Instagram account linking, ad account setup, verification requirements) have enough moving parts that a wrong setup step early on causes problems later — blocked ad accounts, pages that can't be linked properly, or verification applications that get rejected for avoidable reasons.

What We Do

We set up or clean up your Meta Business Portfolio: linking Pages, Instagram accounts, and ad accounts correctly, structuring access for your team, and preparing a business verification application with the documentation Meta typically requires. We guide you through the verification process; we do not control or guarantee Meta's decision.

Use Cases

  • A business setting up Meta Business Suite for the first time and wanting it done right
  • A business whose Pages, Instagram, or ad accounts are messily linked or duplicated and need cleanup
  • A business preparing a verification application that wants to avoid common rejection reasons

How It Works

  1. Audit — review your current Meta Business Portfolio, Pages, and account links.
  2. Fix or set up — correct structure, proper linking, and access permissions for your team.
  3. Prepare verification — gather and check the documentation Meta typically requires, if verification is the goal.
  4. Submit and monitor — submit the application and monitor status; Meta's review and decision timeline is outside our control.

What You Get

A correctly structured Meta Business Portfolio with Pages, Instagram, and ad accounts properly linked, and — if verification is in scope — a submitted application prepared with the documentation Meta typically requires. The verification decision itself is made by Meta.

Who This Is For

Businesses setting up Meta Business Suite for the first time, cleaning up a messy existing setup, or preparing for business verification.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you guarantee my business gets verified?

No. Verification is Meta's decision, based on criteria and a review process we don't control. We prepare your application with the documentation typically required and reduce avoidable rejection reasons, but we cannot guarantee approval.

What's a Business Portfolio?

Meta's structure for managing your Pages, ad accounts, Instagram accounts, and team access in one place. A messy or incorrect setup here causes problems with ads, posting, and verification later.

How long does verification take?

This is set by Meta's own review process, not by us — timelines vary and are outside our control.

Do you also manage ads once setup is done?

Yes, separately — see Meta Ads Management.
