Frequently Asked Questions

What does a competitor intelligence engagement actually deliver?

A structured view of who you're actually competing with, what they're doing in marketing, pricing, and positioning, and where the real gaps and opportunities are.

Is this the same as management consulting?

It's more focused and practical — aimed at founders who need a clear-eyed view of their market and a realistic next step, not a large strategic framework exercise.

Do you guarantee growth results?

No. Growth depends on many factors outside any consultant's control. We provide research, strategy, and honest analysis — not a promised outcome.
