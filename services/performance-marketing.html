Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum budget for Meta Ads or Google Ads in India?

There's no fixed minimum, but ad spend needs to be matched to a realistic testing budget for your industry and city. We work with founders on modest, disciplined weekly budgets rather than recommending a large spend before the fundamentals are proven.

Do you manage the ad account or just advise?

Both — hands-on management, or a one-time audit and strategy if you want to run campaigns in-house. We'll recommend the right fit after understanding your team and goals.

Can you guarantee a specific cost-per-lead or conversion rate?

No — no honest performance marketer can guarantee a specific number before running campaigns. We share realistic ranges once we understand your business and report transparently on actual results.

Do you set up conversion tracking and the Meta Conversion API?

Yes — proper Pixel and Conversion API setup, plus Google Ads conversion tracking, is part of getting an engagement right from day one.
