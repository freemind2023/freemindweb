AI Visibility Audit

Find Out What's Actually Blocking Your Site From AI Search — Before You Fix Anything

A scoped, one-time technical audit of your website's AI-readiness — what's working, what's missing, and a prioritized list of what to fix first. No ongoing commitment required.

Book Free Consultation WhatsApp Us

What does an AI visibility audit check?

Structured data (schema markup) coverage and accuracy, whether AI crawlers can actually access your site, consistency of your business's facts across every page, and whether your key pages answer real user questions directly. It's a technical readiness assessment — not a ranking prediction or a visibility guarantee.

What You Get

  • A written findings report covering structured data, crawler accessibility, and entity consistency
  • A prioritized list of fixes — what matters most, not a generic checklist
  • A clear distinction between what we could verify directly and what remains genuinely unknown
  • No obligation to continue — the audit is a standalone deliverable, not a sales funnel disguised as a report

Who This Is For

Businesses who want an honest, scoped diagnostic before committing to an ongoing AEO/GEO engagement — or who just want to know where they actually stand.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will the audit tell me if I'm currently being cited by ChatGPT or Perplexity?

Where it's possible to check without paid API access or violating a platform's terms, we'll note what we can observe — and we're explicit about the difference between what's verified and what remains unknown.

What do I get at the end of the audit?

A written findings report with prioritized recommendations. If you want implementation, that's a separate AEO & GEO Services engagement — the audit itself is a diagnostic, not a project commitment.

How long does an audit take?

This depends on site size and scope, confirmed during a discovery call — we don't quote a timeline before understanding what we're actually auditing.
