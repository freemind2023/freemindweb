The Problem

Readers who want to know more about an author, or buy their next book, often land on a social media profile with no central place to see every title, a bio, or a reliable purchase link. That friction costs sales and makes it harder to build a following across books.

What We Do

We build a single-author website structured around the actual books: a homepage introducing the author, a book portfolio page per title with cover, description and purchase links, a bio page, and a simple way for readers to get in touch or sign up for updates. The site is built on the same stack Free Mind uses for its own site, so it's fast and straightforward to maintain.

Use Cases

  • An author publishing a new book who wants one link to send press, readers and publishers to
  • An author with several books who currently has no single place listing all of them
  • A first-time author who needs a professional presence before their launch

How It Works

  1. Scope — confirm the author's books, bio, and any existing brand assets (cover images, logo, colors).
  2. Build — a site with a homepage, book portfolio, bio and contact/newsletter signup, using the author's real content.
  3. Review — the author reviews and requests changes before launch.
  4. Launch and handover — the site goes live on the author's own domain, with basic instructions for future updates.

What You Get

A live, author-owned website with a book portfolio, bio page, and purchase links to the author's actual retailers (Amazon, publisher site, or wherever the books are sold). Content is the author's own — we do not write bios or book descriptions on their behalf unless separately asked.

Who This Is For

Published and self-published authors who want one professional, central place for their books and readers to find them.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build?

This depends on how much content (books, bio, images) the author already has ready. A straightforward site with existing content typically takes a few weeks; it's slower if content still needs to be gathered.

Do you write my author bio or book descriptions?

Not by default — we build the site around content the author provides. Ghostwriting or copy support can be arranged separately if needed.

Can the site sell books directly?

The site links out to wherever the author's books are actually sold (Amazon, a publisher's store, etc.). We don't build a separate payment/checkout system as part of this service.

Who owns the website afterward?

The author does. The site is built on the author's own domain and hosting, not a Free Mind-owned platform.

How much does an author website cost?
