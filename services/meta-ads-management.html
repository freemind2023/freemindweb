Meta Ads Management

Meta Ads That Are Actually Managed, Not Just Switched On

Campaign setup, targeting, creative testing, budget optimization, lead generation, and reporting for Facebook and Instagram ads — managed on an ongoing basis, not set up once and left alone.

Book Free Consultation WhatsApp Us

The Problem

A Meta ads account that's switched on and left alone tends to drift — the same tired creative keeps running, budget keeps going to an audience that stopped converting, and nobody notices until spend has been wasted for weeks.

What We Do

We set up campaigns around your actual offer and audience, test creative variations to see what actually performs, adjust targeting and budget based on real results, and report what's working in plain numbers — clicks, cost per lead, conversions — not vanity metrics.

Use Cases

  • A business that has run Meta ads before but isn't sure if the spend is actually working
  • A founder who wants lead generation campaigns managed end to end, not just technically switched on
  • A business that needs creative testing to find what actually converts, instead of guessing

How It Works

  1. Setup — campaign structure, targeting, and initial creative built around your offer.
  2. Launch and test — campaigns go live with creative variations tested against each other.
  3. Optimize — budget and targeting adjusted based on actual performance data, on an ongoing basis.
  4. Report — regular reporting on spend, leads, and cost per result.

What You Get

Live, managed Meta ad campaigns, creative testing, and regular performance reporting. Ad spend itself is paid directly to Meta by you, separate from the management fee.

Who This Is For

Businesses that want Meta ads actively managed and optimized on an ongoing basis, not a one-time campaign setup with no follow-through.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ad spend included in your fee?

No — ad spend is paid directly to Meta by you. Our fee covers setup, management, testing, and reporting.

How much should I budget for ads?

This depends on your goals and industry; we'll give a realistic recommendation during setup rather than a generic number.

How often do you report?

On a regular cadence agreed with you — typically weekly or monthly depending on spend and campaign activity.

Can you also run Google Ads?

Our current ads management focus is Meta (Facebook/Instagram). Ask us directly if you need Google Ads as well.
