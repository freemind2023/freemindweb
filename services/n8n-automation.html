n8n Automation

Your Tools Already Talk to Each Other — They Just Need Someone to Wire It Up

Free Mind designs and builds n8n workflows connecting your CRM, WhatsApp, Google Workspace, and AI tools — so information moves between systems automatically instead of someone copy-pasting between tabs all day.

Book Free Consultation WhatsApp Us

The Problem

A new lead comes in through a form, and someone manually copies it into the CRM, sends a WhatsApp message, and updates a spreadsheet — three separate steps for one event, repeated dozens of times a week, with room for something to be missed.

What We Do

We design and build n8n workflows that connect the tools you already use — CRM, WhatsApp Business, Google Sheets/Workspace, email, and AI models — so one event (a new lead, a form submission, a booking) automatically triggers everything that should happen next, with a human-in-the-loop step wherever it's genuinely needed.

Use Cases

  • A new lead automatically enters your CRM, triggers a WhatsApp welcome message, and logs to a shared sheet
  • Google Workspace automation — a new form response triggers a document, calendar event, or notification
  • Connecting an AI step (like lead qualification or a drafted response) into an existing workflow
  • Migrating an existing Zapier or Make setup to n8n for more flexibility or lower cost at scale

How It Works

  1. Map the current process — what happens manually today, step by step.
  2. Design the workflow — triggers, actions, and where a human should stay in the loop.
  3. Build and test — against real data before it touches production systems.
  4. Deploy and hand over — with documentation, so it's not a black box only we understand.

What You Get

A working, tested n8n workflow connecting your actual tools, documentation of how it works, and — depending on setup — either self-hosted or cloud n8n access so you retain control of the automation.

Who This Is For

Founders and teams doing the same multi-step manual process repeatedly across two or more tools — CRM, WhatsApp, spreadsheets, email — who want it to happen automatically instead.

Example Scenario

Illustrative, not a specific client case: a business collects leads through a website form, then someone manually adds each one to a CRM and sends a WhatsApp follow-up. An n8n workflow could do both automatically the moment the form is submitted — freeing that person's time for actual sales conversations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you have an official n8n partnership?

We use n8n as our primary automation platform for its flexibility and self-hosting option, but we don't claim an official partnership unless one genuinely exists.

What tools can n8n connect to?

Most common business tools with an API — CRMs, Google Sheets/Workspace, WhatsApp Business, email platforms, and AI models.

Can you migrate our existing Zapier or Make workflows to n8n?

Yes — we rebuild the equivalent logic in n8n and test it against your real data before switching over.

Do you self-host n8n or use the cloud version?

Both are options — we recommend based on your data sensitivity, volume, and budget.
