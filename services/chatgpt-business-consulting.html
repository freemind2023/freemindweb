ChatGPT Business Consulting

Most Teams Use 10% of What ChatGPT Can Actually Do for Them

Everyone's tried ChatGPT. Few teams have built it into an actual repeatable workflow for research, content, internal documentation, or customer communication. Free Mind helps Indian business teams go from "we use ChatGPT sometimes" to a documented system the whole team relies on.

The Problem

Individual team members experiment with ChatGPT on their own, get inconsistent results, and never turn it into something the whole team benefits from. There's no shared system, no documented prompts, and no clarity on what's safe to use it for versus what isn't.

What We Do

We work with your team on their actual documents, processes, and terminology to build repeatable ChatGPT workflows — for research, first drafts, internal documentation, meeting summaries, or customer-response drafting — and document them so the system outlasts any one person's habits. Where it genuinely helps, we build a Custom GPT tailored to your business.

Use Cases

  • Research summaries and competitive scans before a strategy meeting
  • First drafts of proposals, emails, and internal documentation
  • Customer-response drafting with a human review step before sending
  • A Custom GPT trained on your product/service details for consistent internal answers

How It Works

  1. Understand — what your team currently does manually, and where ChatGPT fits.
  2. Design — repeatable prompt workflows built around your actual content and terminology.
  3. Train — walk the team through the workflow, not just a generic demo.
  4. Document — a written system the team can reference after we're done.

What You Get

Documented, repeatable ChatGPT workflows for your team's real tasks, team training, and — where it fits — a Custom GPT built around your business. If your needs go further into autonomous, multi-step work, that's our AI Agent Development service.

Who This Is For

Teams already using ChatGPT informally who want to turn scattered individual habits into a shared, documented system — without a large software project.

Example Scenario

Illustrative, not a specific client case: a small marketing team spends hours each week manually summarizing competitor content and industry news before planning. A documented ChatGPT research workflow can cut that to a focused 30-minute review — with the team still doing the actual strategic thinking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this just ChatGPT training, or something more?

Either — a training session, or a deeper workflow build with documented, repeatable prompt systems the whole team uses.

Can ChatGPT help with customer support?

For drafting responses or summarizing tickets, yes, with human review. For a fully automated customer-facing chatbot, see our AI Chatbot Development service.

Will my team's data be safe if we use ChatGPT for business tasks?

We help teams understand what's appropriate to share versus what needs a more controlled setup — this is part of the consulting.

Do you customize ChatGPT for our specific business, or is it generic training?

We build workflows around your actual documents and terminology — including a Custom GPT where useful, not a generic session.
