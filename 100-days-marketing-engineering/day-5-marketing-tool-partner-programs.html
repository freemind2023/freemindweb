30+ Marketing Tool Partner Programs Every Agency Should Know

By Aryan Ahire, Founder — Free Mind Consultancy · Published September 18, 2026 · Part of 100 Days of Marketing Engineering

If you're running a marketing or automation agency and paying full price for every tool, you're leaving money and leverage on the table. Most of the platforms agencies rely on every day — automation, CRM, design, advertising, website, and more — run official partner programs for agencies and consultants. Depending on the platform, that can mean referral commission, discounted plans, free training, certification, priority support, and a public directory listing clients can find you through.

Below is a researched, verified list of real partner programs, organized by category, each linking to the platform's own official page. Terms, tiers, and eligibility change over time and vary by region — treat this as a starting point for research, not a guarantee of what you'll receive, and always confirm current terms on the official page before applying.

A note on accuracy: this list was researched and compiled specifically for this page, with every link checked against each platform's own official site.
