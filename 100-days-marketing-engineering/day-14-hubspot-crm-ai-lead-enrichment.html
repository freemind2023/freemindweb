Your CRM Can Google People Now: HubSpot AI Lead Enrichment

By Aryan Ahire, Founder — Free Mind Consultancy · Published September 29, 2026 · Part of 100 Days of Marketing Engineering

Your CRM can research your leads for you now, for free, the moment they come in. If you're on HubSpot and still opening a new tab to Google every lead by hand, you're spending hours a week on something your CRM can already do automatically.

What's actually happening

HubSpot's AI-powered enrichment fills in company and contact details automatically when a new record is created — things like company size, industry, revenue range and other firmographic data, pulled from available data sources the moment a lead lands in your CRM. No one has to search for it manually.

Automatic enrichment vs. asking a custom question

Lead arrives→ Auto-enriched→ You ask a custom question→ AI finds the answer
  • Automatic enrichment happens by default on qualifying records — standard fields like company size, revenue and industry get filled in without you doing anything.
  • Custom, open-ended questions are the less obvious part: you can ask something like "find this person's LinkedIn profile" or "what industry publications has this company been mentioned in," and HubSpot's AI will go look and return an answer, per lead.

The practical difference is that enrichment gives you structured data fields, while a custom question gives you a specific answer to something you actually need to know before reaching out.

How to set it up

  1. Confirm your HubSpot plan and permissions include AI-powered enrichment — availability depends on your subscription tier.
  2. Go to your contact or company record settings and check which enrichment properties are active.
  3. Test it on a handful of recent leads: look at what got filled in automatically, and how accurate it is against what you already knew.
  4. Try a custom question on one real lead before relying on it for your whole pipeline. Treat early answers as a starting point to verify, not a guarantee.

Where this actually saves time

  • Lead qualification — company size and industry are filled in before a rep even opens the record.
  • Pre-call research — a custom question can surface a LinkedIn profile or recent company news in seconds instead of a five-minute manual search.
  • Consistency — every lead gets the same baseline research instead of it depending on which rep has time to dig.

Read this with healthy skepticism

  • Enrichment data can be wrong or outdated. Automated lookups pull from available sources, which aren't always current. Verify anything that affects a real decision.
  • Not every lead will enrich cleanly. Smaller companies, personal emails or unusual names often return thinner or no results.
  • Check your data and privacy settings. Enrichment pulls from external data sources, so review what your HubSpot plan and your own data policies allow before relying on it for every lead.
