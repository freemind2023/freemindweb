Day 2 / 100Tools

Schedule Content Across Multiple Social Platforms

By Aryan Ahire, Founder — Free Mind Consultancy · Published September 17, 2026 · Part of 100 Days of Marketing Engineering

Tool: Buffer

One of the simplest, highest-leverage habits in social media marketing is scheduling content in advance instead of publishing everything live, in the moment. Buffer is one of the tools built specifically for this.

What Buffer is

Buffer is a social media management tool that lets you plan, schedule, and publish posts across multiple social platforms from a single dashboard, instead of logging into each platform separately every time you want to post.

What problem it solves

Without a scheduling tool, posting consistently across several platforms means manually opening each app, at the right time, every single day — which is exactly the kind of repetitive task that eventually stops happening reliably. Buffer removes the "right time, every day" constraint by letting you prepare posts in batches and queue them to go out automatically.

Why social media teams use scheduling tools

Consistency is one of the few genuinely controllable levers in social media marketing — you can't control the algorithm, but you can control whether you show up regularly. Scheduling tools make consistency achievable without requiring someone to be online at the exact right moment, across every platform, every day.

Basic workflow

Create→ Schedule→ Review→ Publish

In practice: you write or upload the content once, choose the platforms and times it should go out on, do a final review pass (captions, tags, links), and then let the tool publish it automatically at the scheduled time.

Platforms that can be managed

Buffer supports scheduling across the major social platforms marketers commonly use — including Instagram, Facebook, X (Twitter), LinkedIn, and others. Exact platform support and features can change, so the official Buffer website is the accurate, current reference for what's supported.

When Buffer is useful

It's most useful once you're posting on more than one platform, or want to plan content ahead of time (a week or a month) rather than deciding what to post each day. For a single platform posted to occasionally, native scheduling built into that platform may be enough — Buffer's value shows up as the number of platforms and the posting frequency increase.

Free vs. paid functionality

Buffer offers a free plan, with additional features available through paid plans. Exact plan limits and pricing change over time, so treat the official Buffer website below as the source of truth rather than any fixed number here.
