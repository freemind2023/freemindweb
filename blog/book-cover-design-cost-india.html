Book Cover Design Cost in India (2026): Complete Price Guide

By Aryan Ahire, Founder — Free Mind Consultancy

Indian authors consistently underpay for book cover design — and pay for it in poor sales. This guide breaks down every price tier in the Indian market, what you actually get at each level, and the minimum checklist a cover must pass before your book goes live.

Why Your Book Cover Is Your Most Important Marketing Asset

On Amazon India, your book cover is seen as a thumbnail — roughly 100×150 pixels — before any potential reader has read your title, your blurb, or your reviews. A reader's decision to click or scroll past takes under 2 seconds. The cover is the entire pitch at that moment.

Authors who spend Rs. 1,500 on a Canva-generated cover are not saving money. They are paying Rs. 1,500 to make their book invisible. Every sale that does not happen because the cover looked amateurish costs far more than the design saving.

From Aryan Ahire, Free Mind Consultancy: "We have redesigned covers for books that had been live on Amazon for months with zero sales. In nearly every case, the cover was the first problem. After the redesign, sales begin within the first two weeks — not because the book changed, but because readers could finally see it."

What's the Right Budget for Your Book Cover?

The answer depends primarily on your distribution plan:

  • eBook only (Amazon KDP digital): Rs. 5,000–8,000 is sufficient. You only need a front cover, and you don't need print-ready specs. Ensure the cover reads clearly at thumbnail size.
  • Print + eBook: Budget Rs. 8,000–15,000 minimum. Print requires CMYK colour (not RGB), 300 DPI resolution, and a spine sized precisely to your page count. A designer who doesn't know the difference between CMYK and RGB is not qualified to design print covers.
  • Bookstore distribution (IngramSpark, Notion Press, physical retail): Rs. 12,000–25,000. Bookstore quality requires full wrap cover (front + spine + back), precise bleed margins, and files that meet IngramSpark's strict technical specifications.

The 8-Point Cover Quality Checklist

Before approving your book cover from any designer, run it through this checklist:

  • Title is readable at 100×150 pixel thumbnail size (open the file on your phone and check)
  • Front cover resolution is minimum 300 DPI for print, 2560×1600 pixels for Amazon KDP
  • Colour space is CMYK for print files (not RGB — print shops cannot use RGB files)
  • Spine width is calculated from your actual page count (spine formula: pages × 0.0025 inches for standard paper)
  • Back cover includes ISBN barcode placeholder, short blurb, author bio area
  • Bleed area included (usually 3mm all around for offset printing)
  • Source files delivered (PSD, INDD, or AI) — not just the export
  • No copyrighted stock images used without the correct licence
  • Cover uses free Canva templates that appear on dozens of other books (reverse image search the main graphic to check)
  • Designer uses only RGB — cannot produce print-ready files
  • No back cover or spine included — incomplete for print distribution

Common Mistakes Indian Authors Make with Cover Design

Choosing based on price alone

The cheapest option in book cover design is almost always the most expensive decision long-term. A Rs. 2,000 cover that kills your book's visibility across 5 years of Amazon listings costs far more in lost sales than a Rs. 12,000 cover that actually converts browsers to buyers.

Designing before knowing the final page count

If you don't know your final page count, your designer cannot correctly calculate the spine width. A spine that is too wide or too narrow makes the cover unprintable. Always finalise the interior layout before ordering the cover — or at minimum provide the expected page count within 10% accuracy.

Not checking cover at thumbnail size

Every author reviews their cover at full size on a large screen and approves it. Then the book goes live on Amazon and the cover thumbnail looks like coloured noise with unreadable text. Always open the front cover JPEG on your phone at actual display size before approving. If you can't read the title without squinting, your readers can't either.

Accepting a design with no revisions policy

All professional book cover designers include at least 2 to 3 revision rounds. If a designer quotes a price with no revisions, they are not operating a professional service. Always confirm the revision policy in writing before paying.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does book cover design cost in India?

Book cover design in India costs Rs. 1,500 to Rs. 25,000 depending on the designer tier. A budget freelancer charges Rs. 1,500–5,000 for front cover only. An experienced independent designer charges Rs. 5,000–12,000 with revisions and full wrap. A professional publishing agency charges Rs. 8,000–25,000 including print-ready PDF, CMYK colour space, all formats, and source files.

What should a book cover design include?

A professional book cover for print must include: front cover (300 DPI, CMYK), back cover with blurb and ISBN barcode area, spine sized to page count, print-ready PDF, digital JPEG for Amazon listing, and source files. For eBook-only, the minimum is a high-resolution front cover JPEG (2560×1600 pixels minimum).

Can I design a book cover for free in India?

Yes — Canva, Adobe Express, and Amazon KDP Cover Creator are free. However, free tools produce generic covers and cannot deliver print-ready files for physical distribution. For serious commercial publishing, a professional designer is strongly recommended.

How long does book cover design take in India?

5 to 14 working days from brief to final delivery, including 2 to 3 revision rounds. Rush delivery in 3 to 5 days is available for an additional Rs. 2,000 to Rs. 5,000 charge.

What makes a good book cover for Indian markets?

A good cover for Indian markets reads clearly at thumbnail size on a mobile screen, communicates genre instantly through typography and imagery, meets Amazon KDP's technical requirements (2,560×1,600 pixels minimum for front cover), and uses CMYK for print files. The title must be readable at 100×150 pixels — test it on your phone before approving.
